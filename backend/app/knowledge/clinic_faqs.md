# Bright Dental Clinic — Knowledge Base

## About
Bright Dental Clinic is a full-service family dental practice in Mohali, Punjab,
serving patients since 2015. We specialise in painless, single-visit treatments
with digital X-rays and strict sterilisation protocols.

## Timings
- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- Sunday: closed (emergencies by phone only)
- Lunch break: 1:30 PM – 2:30 PM (no appointments)
- Typical appointment length: 30 minutes

## Services and prices
- Routine dental checkup and consultation: ₹300
- Scaling and polishing (cleaning): ₹800
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic, single sitting): ₹4,500
- Root canal treatment (RCT): ₹3,500 to ₹6,000 per tooth depending on the tooth
- Dental crowns: ₹2,500 (metal) to ₹6,500 (zirconia) per crown
- Braces / orthodontics: starting ₹25,000
- Clear aligners: starting ₹45,000
- Dental implants: starting ₹20,000 per tooth
- Kids dentistry consultation: ₹500
- Tooth extraction: ₹500 to ₹2,000 (surgical extra)

## Booking policy
- A ₹500 booking fee confirms every appointment and is adjusted against the
  treatment bill.
- Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before the appointment.
- Late cancellations forfeit the booking fee.
- Approved refunds are processed in 5–7 business days to the original payment
  method.

## Insurance and payments
- We accept cash, UPI, and all major credit/debit cards.
- No-cost EMI available on treatments above ₹10,000.
- We assist with reimbursement paperwork for all major Indian health insurers;
  bring your policy card.

## Preparation instructions
- Root canal: eat a light meal beforehand; do not come on an empty stomach.
- Cleaning: no special preparation needed.
- Whitening: avoid tea, coffee and coloured food for 48 hours after the session.
- Extraction: inform us about blood thinners, diabetes or heart conditions in
  advance.

## Emergencies
For severe pain, swelling, bleeding or trauma, call the clinic directly —
same-day emergency slots are kept open every working day.
